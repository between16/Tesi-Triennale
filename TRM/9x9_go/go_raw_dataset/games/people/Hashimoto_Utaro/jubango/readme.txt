 Game 10 1955-03-28,29  Hashimoto  W+R  180

Total: Hashimoto wins 7-3

Games were played without komi.
After Game 6 the score was 5-1, that is, Hashimoto was 4 games ahead,
and thus had beaten down Fujisawa to sen-ai-sen.
The subsequent four games Fujisawa had B B W B and Hashimoto achieved 2-2.
